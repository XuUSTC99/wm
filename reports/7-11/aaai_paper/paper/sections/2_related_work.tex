\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Physical reasoning benchmarks for world models.}
PhyWorld \citep{kang2024how} generates controlled single-mechanism physics videos (uniform motion, parabola, collision) with explicit in-distribution parameter ranges, and showed that scaled video-diffusion generators memorize cases rather than laws, failing under OOD physics. Physion \citep{bear2021physion} and Physion++ \citep{tung2023physionpp} test object-contact prediction and property inference on realistic simulated video. We connect these two worlds: we use PhyWorld's OOD protocol to dissect \emph{latent} (JEPA-style) world models rather than pixel-space generators, and we validate every surviving conclusion on Physion/Physion++. Our finding that zero-shot transfer from synthetic training is capped by the random-encoder prior extends the benchmark literature with a cautionary calibration: architecture priors alone set a floor that synthetic pretraining fails to exceed.

\paragraph{Physically structured world models.}
A growing line of work argues that world models should carry explicit physical state: physically interpretable world models align latent dimensions with physical quantities through weak supervision \citep{mao2024piwm}, design principles for such models are catalogued by \citet{peper2025principles}, and deep supervision with linear probes injects physical readouts into the training loss \citep{zahorodnii2025deepsup}. Hamiltonian neural networks impose conservation structure directly on the dynamics \citep{greydanus2019hamiltonian}. These works report improved interpretability and reduced drift, typically measured by probe accuracy or latent similarity. We test the transferable core of these proposals---fixed slots, kinematic equations (including the exact gravitational form), probe supervision, and their label-free variants---inside a shared-latent JEPA model, and find all of them harmful under scale-aware metrics. Our analysis attributes the discrepancy to architecture: extrinsic designs make the physical state load-bearing by construction, whereas grafting the same ingredients onto a shared latent leaves them decorative (\S\ref{sec:lbr}).

\paragraph{Rollout training and exposure bias.}
Training autoregressive predictors on their own outputs is a classic remedy for compounding error: scheduled sampling \citep{bengio2015scheduled}, Data-as-Demonstrator \citep{venkatraman2015improving}, and professor forcing \citep{lamb2016professor} all attack the train--test mismatch of teacher forcing, and modern latent world models train on imagined rollouts \citep{ha2018world,hafner2023dreamer}. We claim no novelty for free rollout itself. Our contribution is comparative: under identical, seed-controlled conditions, this training-protocol choice dominates \emph{every} physical-structure intervention across both synthetic and photorealistic-simulation data, and we quantify a horizon--complexity matching law that assigns each domain its optimal rollout length.

\paragraph{Data augmentation for dynamics OOD.}
Physics-informed augmentation has been proposed for vehicle dynamics \citep{jin2024piaug} and world-model robustness \citep{ball2021augmented}. On synthetic domains we confirm augmentation as the strongest OOD lever---appearance jitter halves the hardest-split error---but we document two boundaries this literature has not: augmentation interactions are non-monotone (appearance conflicts with long-horizon training while geometric scaling synergizes with it), and appearance-based gains reverse catastrophically on photorealistic simulation, where appearance carries physical information rather than acting as nuisance. Together with the JEPA representation-learning line \citep{assran2023ijepa,bardes2024vjepa,balestriero2025lejepa}, these results position our study as a systematic empirical audit of what actually confers physical robustness in latent world models.
