\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Physically structured world models.}
A growing line argues that world models should carry explicit physical state: physically interpretable models route prediction through a dedicated low-dimensional state \citep{mao2024piwm,peper2025principles}; deep supervision adds physical readouts to the loss and helps in a low-dimensional state latent, where the supervised quantities occupy ${\sim}38\%$ of the representation \citep{zahorodnii2025deepsup}; closest to ours, Phys-JEPA supervises a decomposed JEPA latent and reports gains \citep{nie2026physjepa}---but on numerical time series, not the visual latents we study. Physics-informed losses \citep{raissi2019pinn}, conservation-structured dynamics \citep{greydanus2019hamiltonian,cranmer2020lnn}, graph simulators over \emph{explicit} particle state \citep{battaglia2016interaction,sanchez2020gns}, and restricted-attention transformers on numerical trajectories \citep{liu2026kepler} share a pattern: structure works when the physical state \emph{is} the representation, not a small part of a shared one. We do not dispute these results in their original settings; we test the transferable core of these proposals inside a shared-latent \emph{visual} JEPA model and find all of them unhelpful or harmful (\S\ref{sec:scan}); our extrinsic port (\S\ref{sec:externalres}) attributes the gap to architecture, not to the physics.

\paragraph{Latent world models and physics benchmarks.}
JEPA-style models predict in representation space \citep{assran2023ijepa,bardes2024vjepa,balestriero2025lejepa,maes2025lewm}; object-level masking structures the JEPA objective rather than the latent's physical content \citep{nam2026causaljepa}; DINO-WM plans over frozen features \citep{zhou2025dinowm,oquab2024dinov2}---our cross-backbone control; Dreamer-line models train on imagined rollouts \citep{ha2018world,hafner2023dreamer}. PhyWorld \citep{kang2024how} supplies controlled physics videos with explicit ID ranges, showing scaled video generators memorize cases rather than laws; Physion/Physion++ \citep{bear2021physion,tung2023physionpp} add realistic---not camera-captured---simulation. We apply PhyWorld's OOD protocol to \emph{latent} models rather than pixel-space generators.

\paragraph{Rollout training and exposure bias.}
Training predictors on their own outputs is a classic remedy for compounding error \citep{bengio2015scheduled,venkatraman2015improving,lamb2016professor}. We claim no novelty for free rollout; its role here is methodological (\S\ref{sec:protocol}): the confound prior injection studies never controlled, and a positive control separating ``structure does not help'' from ``the implementation or metrics are broken.''
