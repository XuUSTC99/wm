\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked whether injecting physics can make a latent world model physically consistent, and answered with a controlled anatomy: injection fails in $26$ of $27$ cells---with the exact gravitational form, with perfect labels, grafted on or co-trained from scratch. The account we defend is that the state is already there: the non-slot dimensions carry the same position, so an injected copy adds no signal while the constraint consumes capacity and gradient---\emph{load-bearing but redundant}. Intervention settles the half we could previously only infer, against our first reading: the injected slot \emph{is} read, by three methods that fail in different ways, while the black box keeps its own copy. Re-weighting reaches only parity; an architecture that removes the redundancy does help, until an OOD shift reaches its encoder---necessary, not sufficient. What moved prediction was never the structure but the training protocol, worth $2.2$--$8.3\times$ on identical code and metrics.

The constructive reading: an inductive bias must supply what the latent is missing---\emph{how the state evolves, not what the state is}. Two routes stay open: injections that target dynamics or conserved quantities rather than state, and extrinsic designs paired with OOD-robust encoders. Our own evolution-targeting variants are deliberately simple---a kinematic update and a consistency prior on slot coordinates---so their failure bounds that class, not evolution injection in general: collision turns on contact and inter-object relation, which only operator-level designs (symplectic, learned contact) can express.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Our conclusions rest primarily on one compact backbone (${\sim}10$M ViT-tiny JEPA); the headline findings replicate on frozen DINOv2, where injection is inert rather than harmful---so the \emph{harm} claim is established on the trainable-encoder regime. All scan cells and the $2{\times}2$ are three-seed; the Physion++ variants remain single-seed, the probe variants untested there. On the mechanism, the slot's use is demonstrated; the black box's role stays partly inferential---erasing its position copy takes $116$--$154$ of $190$ dimensions, at which rank amnesic projection loses selectivity (a limit we quantify, not a step we skipped), and wholesale replacement passes its controls in one of six cells.
